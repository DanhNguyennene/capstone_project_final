\section{Introduction}

% ── 1. Motivation, Goals, and Scope ──────────────────────────────────
\begin{frame}{Motivation and Goals}
  Slurm powers $>$50\% of TOP500 clusters~\cite{yoo2003slurm} yet remains inaccessible to most users.
  State-changing operations affect all cluster users, and sensitive workload metadata cannot leave the site.
  \vspace{0.6em}
  \begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{7pt}
    \item \textbf{Natural-language interface} for broad Slurm operations --- inspect jobs, nodes, partitions, accounting, and submit/control workloads in plain English.
    \item \textbf{Safety-enforcing dual-agent architecture} --- Observer (read-only) and Operator (write) with structural tool partitioning and mandatory HITL confirmation.
    \item \textbf{Curated benchmark} --- 3,135-case dataset across 11 Slurm categories with ground-truth labels for tool use, routing, HITL, and state transitions.
    \item \textbf{Domain-adapted fine-tuning} --- QLoRA on Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct distilled from commercial traces; local deployment, no external API dependency.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{frame}
