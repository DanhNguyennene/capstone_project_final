% ── Gap Summary ──────────────────────────────────────────
\begin{frame}{Research Gap}
  \begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{8pt}
    \item \textbf{HPC systems} lack NL interfaces, tool-calling, and fine-tuning --- users must know CLI syntax.
    \item \textbf{General tool-calling agents} (Gorilla, ToolLLM, AgentTuning) have no safety enforcement for destructive ops and no domain-specific evaluation.
    \item \textbf{Domain benchmarks} ($\tau$-bench) provide no domain-adapted local model and no confirmation gate.
    \item \textbf{To our knowledge, no prior work} combines all six: NL~+~typed Slurm tools~+~HITL~+~domain FT~+~local deploy~+~domain benchmark.
  \end{itemize}
  \vspace{0.5em}
  \centering
  $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{This thesis fills the gap.}
\end{frame}
