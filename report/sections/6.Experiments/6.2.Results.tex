\subsection{Failure Pattern Analysis}

There were no runtime execution errors in this benchmark (0/455 tests with \texttt{error} field set). Most remaining failures are therefore behavioral mismatches rather than infrastructure failures. Because a single failed test can contain multiple mismatch types, the counts below are non-exclusive.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \caption{Error Analysis Across Failed Curated-Benchmark Cases}
    \label{tab:mismatch-counts}
    \begin{tabular}{|p{4cm}|c|p{7cm}|}
        \hline
        \textbf{Error Type} & \textbf{Failed Cases} & \textbf{Interpretation} \\
        \hline
        Wrong tool selection & 19 & The agent selected or exposed a tool inconsistent with ground truth, especially in release/update/submission cases. \\
        Wrong handoff/routing & 4 & The Observer/Operator boundary was not followed as expected. \\
        Unsafe action blocked or HITL mismatch & 14 & The behavior either blocked/refused an action unexpectedly or failed to match the expected confirmation pattern. \\
        Clarification needed & 12 & The interaction required a clearer target or wording than the benchmark expected. \\
        Keyword/scorer issue & 17 & The executed behavior and response evidence were not aligned with strict keyword/scoring expectations. \\
        \hline
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Across all 455 cases, structural mismatch counts were: tool recall $<1.0$ in 41 cases, routing mismatch in 6 cases, HITL mismatch in 23 cases, state mismatch in 20 cases, and keyword score $<0.6$ in 38 cases. The failed-case table highlights where these mismatches affected pass/fail outcomes.

\subsection{Latency Characteristics}

Average end-to-end latency is 22.59 seconds. This reflects full agent-tool execution with judge-enabled evaluation and is more representative of architecture-level evaluation cost than of direct CLI latency.

\subsection{Manual Scenario Validation Summary}

Manual runs were used to validate user-facing behavior not fully captured by scalar metrics:

\begin{itemize}
    \item chat responsiveness and stream readability,
    \item confirmation flow clarity for destructive operations,
    \item correctness of inline Mermaid artifacts,
    \item usability of the integrated evaluation dashboard.
\end{itemize}

These manual checks confirmed the expected interaction flow in both chat mode and evaluation mode.

\subsection{Result Interpretation}

The current system performs strongly on the curated benchmark and demonstrates improved cross-scenario stability. However, the result should not be presented as proof of unrestricted real-world robustness. The next reliability question is whether the same behavior holds on the held-out paraphrase split and later on live-cluster prompts written by users outside the dataset design process.
