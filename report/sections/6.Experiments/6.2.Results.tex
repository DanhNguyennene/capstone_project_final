\subsection{Failure Pattern Analysis}

There were no runtime execution errors in this benchmark (0/367 tests with \texttt{error} field set). Most failures are therefore behavioral mismatches rather than infrastructure failures.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \caption{Mismatch Counts Across 367 Cases}
    \label{tab:mismatch-counts}
    \begin{tabular}{|l|c|}
        \hline
        \textbf{Mismatch Type} & \textbf{Count} \\
        \hline
        Tool recall $< 1.0$ & 109 \\
        Routing mismatch & 62 \\
        HITL mismatch & 64 \\
        State mismatch & 71 \\
        Keyword score $< 0.6$ & 20 \\
        \hline
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Among 130 failed tests, structural mismatches dominate: tool selection/coverage (109), state mismatch (71), and routing/HITL mismatches (62/64). This indicates that architecture-level reliability, not surface-level wording, is the key improvement target.

\subsection{Latency Characteristics}

Average end-to-end latency is 12.92 seconds. Category-level averages indicate higher latency in \texttt{submission}, \texttt{safety}, and \texttt{multi\_step} workflows (roughly 16--17 seconds), consistent with additional reasoning and safety checks.

\subsection{Manual Scenario Validation Summary}

Manual runs were used to validate user-facing behavior not fully captured by scalar metrics:

\begin{itemize}
    \item chat responsiveness and stream readability,
    \item confirmation flow clarity for destructive operations,
    \item correctness of inline Mermaid artifacts,
    \item usability of the integrated evaluation dashboard.
\end{itemize}

These manual checks confirmed the expected interaction flow in both chat mode and evaluation mode.

\subsection{Comparison with Manual CLI Workflow}

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \caption{Task Complexity: Agent vs Manual CLI}
    \label{tab:agent-vs-cli}
    \begin{tabular}{|p{4cm}|c|c|}
        \hline
        \textbf{Task} & \textbf{Agent Interaction} & \textbf{Typical Manual CLI} \\
        \hline
        Check queue status & 1 prompt & 1--2 commands \\
        Diagnose pending jobs & 1 prompt & 3--5 commands + interpretation \\
        Cluster utilization summary & 1 prompt (+ optional chart) & 4--6 commands + manual synthesis \\
        Safe cancellation flow & 2 turns (request + confirm) & 1 command (no built-in conversational guardrail) \\
        \hline
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The agent offers the largest benefit in multi-source interpretation tasks, while direct read-only queries are closer to command-line effort.

\subsection{Result Interpretation}

The current system is strong in read-oriented and account-oriented assistance, and demonstrates good cross-scenario stability. However, the benchmark also shows clear reliability gaps in complex multi-step and safety-sensitive flows. These findings directly inform the limitation and future-work priorities in Chapter~7.
