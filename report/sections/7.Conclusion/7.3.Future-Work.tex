\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:future-work}

\subsection{Reliability and Real-Cluster Validation}

The benchmark identifies the largest remaining gaps in \textit{submission} (83.4\%), \textit{docs} (86.7\%), and \textit{multi\_step} (83.3\%) categories. Near-term work should target tool-routing consistency for multi-step workflows, HITL mismatch cases in safety-sensitive tasks, and state-transition success for submission flows, measured against the pinned model stack.

Beyond synthetic benchmarking, real-cluster validation is the most critical external validity step. A controlled deployment on a live Slurm cluster would expose scheduler variability, concurrent user interactions, and operational failure modes that the fixed mock-state scenarios cannot replicate. This validation is necessary before drawing strong conclusions about generalisability beyond the five snapshot states used in training and evaluation.

\subsection{Security and Deployment Readiness}

Production deployment in multi-tenant HPC environments requires capabilities not exercised by the current evaluation harness: role-aware authentication and authorisation, auditable action logs for safety-critical operations, and policy controls for high-impact tool invocations such as job cancellation at scale. These controls are prerequisites for institutional adoption and should be addressed before any live deployment.

\subsection{Extended Evaluation}

The benchmark evaluates single-turn interactions exclusively. Real users interact across multiple turns---asking follow-up questions, refining requests, and referencing prior context. Extending the dataset to ordered turn sequences with per-turn ground truth, and modifying the evaluation runner to maintain session state across turns, would enable metrics for context retention and conversational routing consistency. A related gap is the web-search fallback path: web results are non-reproducible due to page drift and ranking changes, so the current evaluation excludes web-dependent cases. A cached snapshot mechanism for search results would make this path evaluable.

\subsection{Open-Weight Model Parity}

The fine-tuning demonstrates that role-scoped distillation closes 29\% of the gap to the commercial baseline, but a residual capacity difference remains. Closing this further involves training on larger and more diverse trace corpora including failure-recovery examples, applying preference optimisation (DPO) on cases where the fine-tuned model diverges from expected behaviour, and evaluating larger base models as they become available. The long-term goal is a fully self-hosted deployment that matches commercial-API quality while eliminating external dependencies and data-sovereignty concerns.
